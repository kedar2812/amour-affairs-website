What We Offer

The Film Experience

Highlight Film
4–6 Minutes · 4K

The emotion, the music, the moments that defined your day — distilled into a cinematic short film. Set to custom licensed music, colour-graded to a warm romantic tone. The one you'll watch on every anniversary.

Documentary Film
60–90 Minutes · 4K

The full uncut story — vows in their entirety, every speech, candid tears, every laugh, and every slow dance. Shot on a 2-camera setup with lapel mics for pristine audio. The complete record of your day.

Same-Day Edit
3–5 Minutes · Played at Reception

Played at your reception the very same evening — a surprise film of your morning, your ceremony, your tears and your joy. The ultimate wedding surprise that makes the whole room stop and feel.

How We Work

Our Process

Pre-Shoot Call

We sit down to understand your soundtrack, your visual style, and your vision. What feeling should your film evoke? We build the entire edit around that answer.

Wedding Day

2-camera setup, lapel microphones on the couple and officiant, and drone b-roll of your venue and landscapes — all captured unobtrusively while you simply live your day.

Edit & Colour Grade

Every frame is colour graded to a warm cinematic look. Custom licensed music — chosen specifically for your story. The edit goes through multiple rounds of review before it reaches you.

Delivery

A private Vimeo link + 4K download within 8 weeks of your wedding date. Share it with the world, or keep it just for the two of you. It's yours, forever.
