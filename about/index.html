Taher Husain

Founder & Creative Director
"He doesn't shoot weddings. He archives the exact second two people stop performing and simply exist together."

Taher Husain approaches weddings with a rare kind of clarity — the kind that comes from knowing what matters, and more importantly, what doesn't.

Since founding Amour Affairs in 2011, he has built a body of work that is understated, intentional, and deeply personal. His images are not shaped by trends or theatrics. They are shaped by observation, timing, and an instinct for the quiet truth in human connection.

There is a deliberate restraint in the way Taher works. He doesn't interrupt moments or manufacture them. He waits for them — and captures them at their most unguarded. The result is imagery that feels effortless, yet carries a distinct weight of permanence.

Over the years, Amour Affairs has quietly earned the trust of every couple it has worked with. It is a consistency that speaks for itself. In an industry often defined by noise, the brand has made its mark through discretion, reliability, and an uncompromising commitment to the work.

Taher's personal philosophy is simple, but deeply felt:

The more you give, the happier you live.

It shows — not just in how he works, but in what he leaves behind. Photographs that continue to hold meaning, long after the moment has passed.
