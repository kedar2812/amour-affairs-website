Cookies

Our website makes use of Cookies in a purely harmless way and solely in order to make your browsing experience seamless. We make use of two types of Cookies — 'session' Cookies that remove themselves from your device when you close your browser, and 'persistent' Cookies that remain on your device until deleted manually or they reach their expiration date.

We use Cookies harmlessly for purposes such as recognising that you are re-visiting our site and hence to load static elements quicker, to improve the usability and security of the site, as part of our analytics platform, and to serve you content that is more specific to you. No personal information is stored in the process.

Information provided by the end user or visitor may be used for Amour Affairs's promotional campaigns. A user may opt out of our database by using the 'Unsubscribe' details provided in the promotional e-mails or by sending us an email at info@amouraffairs.in.
