Pune Wedding Photography Checklist01 / Before the Wedding
Before the Wedding

Photography Planning Checklist

Settle these early. Most last-minute photography stress traces back to one of them.

  • Finalise your wedding photography style
  • Decide which functions need photography coverage
  • Confirm wedding dates and timings
  • Share venue details with your photographer
  • Prepare a list of important family/group photographs
  • Mention any special traditions or moments you want captured
  • Discuss the photography timeline with your photographer
  • Confirm the number of photographers required

Getting Ready

Don’t Miss These Details

The quiet hours before the ceremony often give you the most personal photographs of the day.

  • Jewellery and accessories
  • Invitation cards

  • Friends and bridesmaids/groomsmen

Pune Wedding Photography Checklist04 / Family & Candid
Family & Candid Moments

The Moments You Don’t Want to Miss

The people around you are half the story. Make sure they are in it.

  • Immediate family photographs
  • Grandparents and elders
  • Friends and cousins
  • Natural conversations
  • Emotional moments
  • Fun moments between guests
  • Group photographs
  • Unplanned candid moments
TipAsk one relative who knows both families to help gather people for group photographs. It keeps them quick.

Pune Wedding Photography Checklist05 / Couple Portraits
Couple Portraits

Plan Time for Your Couple Session

Portraits need a little protected time in the schedule. Without it, they are usually the first thing to get squeezed.

  • Decide whether you prefer natural, candid or posed portraits
  • Choose suitable locations at the venue
  • Keep enough time between ceremonies
  • Consider golden-hour portraits if the schedule allows
  • Include both intimate and fun photographs
  • Don’t forget a few photographs with the venue/environment

Pune Wedding Photography Checklist06 / Before You Book
Before You Book

Questions to Ask Your Wedding Photographer

Before booking, ask:

  1. What photography style do you specialise in?
  2. Can we see a complete wedding gallery?
  3. How many photographers will cover our wedding?
  4. How many hours of coverage are included?
  5. What exactly is included in the package?
  6. Do you provide wedding cinematography?
  7. Are albums included?
  8. How does the booking process work?
  9. What is the expected delivery process?
  10. Do you provide a contract?
