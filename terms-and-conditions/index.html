Terms & Conditions

Everything that governs our agreement with you, in plain language.

"These terms govern our agreement with you and ensure a smooth, transparent experience for everyone involved in your wedding journey."

  1. 01
    Pre-Event Consultation

    The parties agree to a consultation prior to 30 days before the event to finalise shooting times and locations. This ensures we are perfectly aligned with your vision and can plan every detail of your day.

  2. 02
    Booking Fee & Payment Terms

    A booking fee of 50% secures the date. This advance is non-refundable and non-transferable. The balance payment is due before the last day of the wedding. 18% GST is applicable on all services.

  3. 03
    Cancellation & Postponements

    Postponed weddings retain the booking fee provided the photographer is able to re-schedule to the new date. Cancellations — regardless of reason — are non-refundable as the date is blocked exclusively for your event.

  4. 04
    Delivery

    Pictures and videos will be delivered within 60–90 working days after receipt of full payment. Sneak peeks and couple-shot previews are typically delivered within one week of the event.
